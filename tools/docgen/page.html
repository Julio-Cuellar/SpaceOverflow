NASA Space Apps 2026 · astronaut health challenge

Health monitor architecture plan

Modular monolith with hexagons per module, an event bus with a PostgreSQL outbox, and three channels with one job each: REST for machines, GraphQL for the interface and WebSocket for what the server pushes. Works without internet.

    Data model

    ER diagram per module

    One PostgreSQL schema per module. Foreign keys live inside the module. Between modules there are only soft references by id. Select a table to see its relations.

    User experience

    Journey and prototype

    Pick a stage of the journey. The prototype uses the real PIO₂, ppCO₂, total pressure and rate-of-change rules from the catalog. The yellow bands and the body values are demo data and carry the label.

    Cabin conditions

    Ship panel

    Floor plan, incidents and atmosphere by zone

    Readings are simulated every second in each zone. Trigger an anomaly and watch the zone change color, the ticket appear with its description, severity and location, and the tasks get created. The initial example already has high CO₂ in the lab.

    Mission control · option B

    Task plan from Ground

    Ground publishes versioned plans with health and ship tasks. The ship applies them, keeps the last one if the link drops and lets the crew create their own tasks or block the Ground ones with a reason. Cut the link, publish a plan and watch it queue up and arrive later.
